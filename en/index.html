Shigeaki Yokota is a Project Researcher at Osaka University working in metric measure geometry.

Contact: yokota.shigeaki.fat@osaka-u.ac.jp

Research overview

I study the geometry of spaces equipped with distances and probability measures, focusing on concentration, the geometry of data, and limits of high-dimensional spaces.

Measure concentration and pyramids

I study the limit formula for observable diameter, as well as limits and compactifications of spaces through pyramids.

Geometric Data Sets

I study the geometry of Geometric Data Sets, including their pyramids and compactifications.

High-dimensional spaces and Gaussian limits

I study Gaussian pyramids and the limits and phase transitions of measures on hyperbolic spaces. I also write about the relationship between high-dimensional spheres and Gaussian distributions.
